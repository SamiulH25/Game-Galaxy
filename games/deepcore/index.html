Halcyon Deep salvage guild

Deepcore

A derelict mining station is sinking into a gas giant. Dive ten decks, scavenge what you can, and light the Core's escape beacon.

How to play
  • Everything is turn-based: enemies act only after you do.
  • Move: tap a tile, or arrows / WASD / numpad / hjklyubn (diagonals). Bump an enemy to attack.
  • Wait: tap yourself, Space or . · Descend: stand on the lift and press >.
  • Items: 1–6 or the bar below. Grenades and blinks ask for a target.
  • Arc Pistol: press F (or Fire) to shoot; it needs energy cells.
  • Fabricators (amber terminals) trade scrap for supplies. Level-ups grant upgrades.
