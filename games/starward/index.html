Starward

Carry the beacon core across three hostile sectors. Build your deck, outfit your ship, and survive the Eclipse.

How to play
  • Pick a path across each sector map: battles, elites, events, shops and repair docks.
  • Each turn you get 3 Energy and draw 5 cards. Play cards, then end your turn.
  • Enemies show their intent — plan Shield against incoming attacks.
  • Shield resets each turn. Win fights to add cards and modules to your ship.
  • Beat a run to unlock higher Threat levels for bigger scores.
