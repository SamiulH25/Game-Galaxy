⚗ Midnight Alchemist — How to Play

  • Day (hours 1–6): Shop closed. Double-click pantry ingredients to extract (costs gold).
  • Night (hours 7–12): Customers arrive. Brew potions and sell from stock.
  • Brewing: Select ingredient → click cauldron to load (up to 3). Press Brew. Match a codex recipe or get Foul Sludge.
  • Bottle finished brews before they spoil (20s) unless you buy Insulated Glass Vials.
  • Customers: Sell matching stock, Haggle (50/50), or Turn Away. Candle = patience.
  • Rank rises with lifetime gold, unlocking ingredients.
  • Guild License required for tier-3 recipes (Venom Ward through Sovereign Remedy).
